Why Buildings Have Harmonics

A perfect power grid would deliver a pure sine wave. But every non-linear load — LED drivers, switching power supplies, variable-speed drives, computers — distorts the waveform, injecting harmonics.


Odd harmonics (3rd, 5th, 7th...) dominate because most loads are symmetrical. The 3rd harmonic is especially problematic — it adds up in the neutral wire instead of canceling.


Different buildings have different harmonic signatures. A data center full of switching PSUs looks very different from a home with mostly resistive loads.
