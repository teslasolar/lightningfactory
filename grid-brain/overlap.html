What's Happening

Power grids operate at 50 Hz or 60 Hz, but they don't just produce a clean sine wave. Every wire, transformer, and appliance generates harmonics — multiples of the base frequency.


Your brain produces electrical oscillations in named bands:


Delta (0.5–4 Hz) · Theta (4–8 Hz) · Alpha (8–13 Hz) · Beta (13–30 Hz) · Gamma (30–100 Hz)


The gamma band (30–100 Hz) is where your brain's frequencies overlap with the grid's fundamental and lower harmonics. This tool shows you exactly where those overlaps occur.
