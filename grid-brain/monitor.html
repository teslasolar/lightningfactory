What You're Hearing

Every room has an acoustic signature. Transformers, fluorescent ballasts, refrigerators, HVAC systems — they all emit tones at the grid frequency or its harmonics.

The fundamental hum is usually at 100 or 120 Hz (double the grid frequency) because transformers vibrate at twice the electrical frequency due to the magnetic force being proportional to B².


This tool uses your microphone to perform a real-time FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) and identify the dominant frequencies in your room. Look for spikes at:

• 50/60 Hz — Direct grid hum (rare acoustically)

• 100/120 Hz — Transformer vibration (most common)

• 150/180 Hz — 3rd harmonic

• 200/240 Hz — 4th harmonic
